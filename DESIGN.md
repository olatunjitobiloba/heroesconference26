# Heroes Conference Raffle Design

## Visual Thesis
Black stone, brushed gold, and stage-light contrast: the raffle should feel like a ceremonial reveal for "Adullam: Birthplace of Kings," not a generic lottery widget.

## Product Context
- Product: Event raffle draw website.
- Event: Heroes Conference '26, Covenant University Chapel, June 7, 3 PM.
- Theme: Adullam: Birthplace of Kings.
- Audience: Live conference operators and attendees watching a big screen.
- Primary goal: Draw one or more numbers from 1 to 1000 with a dramatic real-time reveal.

## Style Sources
- 70% premium conference stage graphics: deep black, monumental type, gold lighting, high contrast.
- 20% broadcast control room: clear status, large readable numbers, operator-safe controls.
- 10% ceremonial biblical texture: stone, cave shadow, crown-gold accents.

## Tokens
- Background: `#050505`, `#0b0a07`, `#16120a`.
- Gold: `#f4d06f`, `#c9972c`, `#fff3bb`.
- Text: `#fff8df`, `#bfb8a2`, `#776f5d`.
- Alert: `#e23b2e` for date emphasis only.
- Radius: 8px on controls, 6px on compact elements.
- Spacing: 8px grid with 16, 24, 32, 48, 64.
- Typography: Impact-style condensed display for numbers and headings, system sans for controls.

## Layout Rules
- The draw number owns the screen.
- Controls sit in a quiet operator rail and never compete with the reveal.
- Speaker and event assets act as atmosphere, not decoration.
- Big-screen mode should remain readable from a distance.
- Mobile keeps all controls accessible without hiding the draw.

## Motion
- Number roll accelerates then locks into the winning number.
- Gold particles burst on reveal.
- Background video stays subtle under a dark overlay.
- Buttons and winner rows transition in 150-300ms.

## Content Rules
- Use direct event language.
- Avoid explaining how to use the app inside the interface.
- Keep the theme visible: Heroes Conference, Adullam, Birthplace of Kings, CU Chapel, June 7, 3 PM.

## SOUL
The page should feel like the moment a name is called from a stage. It should carry the cave-to-crown tension of Adullam without turning solemn or heavy. Every reveal should feel clean, credible, and worth watching.
